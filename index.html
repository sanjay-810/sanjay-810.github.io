Sanjay Bhargav


I will be joining the University of Trento as a PhD student in Information Engineering and Computer Science, where I will be working with Prof. Nicu Sebe. My research interests are broadly in 3D computer vision, geometric vision, neural rendering, generative models, and learning-based scene understanding.

I am currently a Research Fellow at the Center for Visual Information Technology (CVIT), International Institute of Information Technology Hyderabad (IIIT-H), under the supervision of Prof. C. V. Jawahar and Dr. Zakaria Laskar. My work focuses on 3D scene representation, novel-view synthesis, and neural rendering for challenging real-world driving environments.

Previously, I was a Research Intern at the Saab-NTU Joint Lab, Singapore, where I worked on 3D object detection, domain adaptation, and continual learning using LiDAR and camera sensors. I completed my five-year Integrated Master's degree in Physics from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India.

For any inquiries, feel free to reach out to me via mail!
